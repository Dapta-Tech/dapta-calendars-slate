Settings > Members keeps each member's name and email readable from tablet width up: the identity keeps a minimum width and the controls wrap below it, where the controls used to take the whole line and leave an empty row (most visible in Spanish). A long name truncates with its full text in the title. A resend that hits the workspace's daily invitation limit now shows the page's own message, in the reader's language, instead of the API's English text. A team event's Hosts block names the hosts who receive no bookings yet because they have no working hours (and, in a collective event, says no times are offered at all), and the hosts with no calendar connected, whose busy times are not checked and who get nothing written to a calendar of their own.
